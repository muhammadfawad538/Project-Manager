## Escalation Criteria

A risk is escalated to the steering committee when:
- Impact is **High** AND probability is **Medium or High**
- Contingency budget of **>$N** would be required
- Risk threatens a **critical milestone** within the next {N} days
- The risk owner cannot resolve within **{N} business days**
